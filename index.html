About Me

Hello! I am Bowen Chai (柴博文). I am a first-year master's student at the School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Danping Zou. I received my bachelor's degree from SJTU in 2026. During my undergraduate studies, I interned in Prof. Yulun Zhang's lab.

My research interest is computer vision, including diffusion models, 3D vision (e.g., depth estimation), image/video restoration, and model compression (e.g., quantization).
